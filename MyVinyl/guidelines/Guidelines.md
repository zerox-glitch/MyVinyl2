# Vynyl Record — prototype guidelines
Stance: warm vintage art-deco, committed. Obsidian ground with amber/mesh glow, brass hairlines, cream text, ruby labels.
Type: Gloock (display serif), Hanken Grotesk (body), Big Shoulders Display (deco caps labels, wide tracking), DM Mono (durations/status only).
Tokens live in src/index.css @theme. Panels: #211E1A, 1px brass/20 border, radius 18px. Accent amber used only for primary actions and selected states.
